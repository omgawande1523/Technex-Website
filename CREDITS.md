# 3D Asset & Model Credits — TECHNEX 2026–27 Events Particle System

This project combines scanned 3D GLB models with original algorithmic procedural geometries to sample 40,000 points per form with curvature-weighted edge distribution for GPGPU simulation.

## Original Procedural Form Generators
The following 10 forms are custom-authored procedural geometries designed specifically to be fully original (with no existing trademarked film/comic designs):
1. **Powered Exosuit**: Custom segmented chest-plate lattice, floating micro-core reactor, articulated shoulder pauldrons, and sleek visor.
2. **Ship's Computer Console**: Curved retro-futuristic bridge desk, dual angled HUD display planes, tiered button banks, and an orbiting holosphere.
3. **Miniature Submarine**: Convex observation dome, cylindrical pressure hull, lateral searchlights, manipulator arms, and twin stern propellers.
4. **Tactical Drone**: X-chassis carbon arms, 4 ducted rotor bells, central avionics pod, and downward optical gimbal.
5. **Orbital Rocket**: Parabolic aerodynamic fuselage, triple swept stabiliser fins, and bell thruster nozzle.
6. **Neural Circuit Chip**: QFP microprocessor body, 32 perimeter copper/gold lead pins, center silicon die, and etched security buses.
7. **Edison Light Bulb**: Teardrop vacuum glass bulb, brass screw threads, and glowing dual-loop tungsten/carbon filament.
8. **Articulated Robotic Arm**: Rotating turret base, dual hydraulic knuckle pivots, articulated forearm, and 3-finger gripper.
9. **Supersonic Jet**: Delta-wing interceptor, needle nosecone, angled twin vertical stabilizers, and afterburner exhaust bells.
10. **Precision Spur Gear**: 24-tooth involute spur gear with 5 circular weight-relief apertures, rim bevels, and center keyed axle hub.
11. **Suspension Bridge**: Twin gothic truss pylons, parabolic catenary suspension cables, vertical wire suspenders, and roadway deck.
